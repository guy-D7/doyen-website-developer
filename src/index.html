How a visitor becomes a customer

Every visitor asks 4 questions before they contact you. Each part of your site should answer one.

  1. 1

    "What is this?"

    A clear headline, real photos and a page that loads fast.

  2. 2

    "Is this for me?"

    Copy about their problem, in their words, easy to scan on a phone.

  3. 3

    "Can I trust them?"

    Reviews, photos, prices and your process shown up front.

  4. 4

    "How do I start?"

    One obvious next step — call, WhatsApp, book or buy.

Answer all four and more of the same visitors turn into enquiries.
